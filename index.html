Commissions

How to get your own sketch

Commissioning a piece is simple, and you stay involved from the first line to the last.

1

Send your reference

Fill in the booking form with a clear photo, your preferred canvas size and any details you want.

2

Confirm & review

Once I agree to a slot I will ask for payment. While drawing, I share work-in-progress reviews so it comes out just right.

3

Receive your artwork

Delivery usually takes 1 week to 1 month, depending on complexity. Three rounds of changes are included.
